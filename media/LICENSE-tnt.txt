TNT and gunpowder: textures and sounds from Minetest Game's tnt mod
(https://github.com/luanti-org/minetest_game, mods/tnt), unchanged and under
their original names:

  tnt_top.png, tnt_bottom.png, tnt_side.png, tnt_top_burning_animated.png,
  tnt_tnt_stick.png, tnt_gunpowder_inventory.png,
  tnt_gunpowder_straight.png, tnt_gunpowder_curved.png,
  tnt_gunpowder_t_junction.png, tnt_gunpowder_crossing.png,
  tnt_gunpowder_burning_straight_animated.png,
  tnt_gunpowder_burning_curved_animated.png,
  tnt_gunpowder_burning_t_junction_animated.png,
  tnt_gunpowder_burning_crossing_animated.png,
  tnt_boom.png, tnt_smoke.png, tnt_blast.png (a blast's particles),
  tnt_explode.ogg, tnt_ignite.ogg, tnt_gunpowder_burning.ogg
